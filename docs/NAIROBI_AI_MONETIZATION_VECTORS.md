## Executive Summary
The Kenyan technology ecosystem is currently undergoing two massive structural transitions:
1. **The Open Finance Transition:** Traditional banking walled gardens and M-Pesa APIs are opening up to third-party micro-integrations.
2. **Offline-to-Cloud Telecom Bridging:** 80%+ of the regional consumer and SME base operates with intermittent mobile data. Whoever provides high-leverage AI capability via zero-data cellular protocols captures untapped economic market share.

## 1. Vector #1: Autonomous Open Finance & M-Pesa Ledger Reconciliation API
- **The Market Pain:** Kenyan merchants, e-commerce stores, and SACCOs suffer high transaction failure rates, orphaned payments, and missing withdrawal reconciliations between bank core systems and M-Pesa.
- **The Solution:** A lightweight, idempotent Webhook Daemon (similar to our Supabase-backed event queue) that automatically detects dropped transactions, issues automated ledger reconciliations, and sends SMS alerts.
